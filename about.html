Who We Are

More Than a Gym — A Lifestyle

PowerZone Fitness Club was founded in 2014 by a team of certified athletes and wellness coaches with one mission: make world-class fitness accessible to everyone. What started as a small 2,000 sqft studio in Pune's Shivaji Nagar has grown into a nationwide network of premium fitness centres.

We believe fitness isn't a destination — it's a daily journey. Our holistic approach combines cutting-edge equipment, evidence-based programs, certified trainers, and a vibrant community that celebrates every milestone.

From beginners nervous about their first workout, to competitive athletes chasing podium finishes — PowerZone is built for you.

Our Journey

A Decade of Growth

2014

🏗️ The Beginning

Founded in a 2,000 sqft studio in Pune with 3 trainers and 50 founding members who believed in the dream.

2016

🏊 Aqua Wing Launches

Opened our first Olympic-size pool facility, expanding our aqua fitness and swimming lesson programs.

2017

📈 5-Branch Milestone

Expanded to Mumbai, Nashik, and Nagpur, growing to 5 branches and 10,000 active members.

2020

💻 Digital Pivot

Launched PowerZone Online during the pandemic, delivering live and on-demand classes to 20,000+ members at home.

2022

🏆 National Award

Recognized as "India's Best Fitness Brand" by Sports Authority of India for excellence in community fitness.

2024 — Present

🚀 Nation-Wide Expansion

Now serving 8 cities with 12 centres, 60,000+ members, and a dedicated mobile app for seamless fitness management.
