Our Story

Built by Athletes, For Everyone

PowerZone was born in 2014 with a single dumbbell rack and a dream. Today, we're one of India's fastest-growing fitness chains with 12 branches and a community of 60,000+ members.

Our philosophy is simple: every body deserves world-class fitness. Whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned athlete, our doors are open.

2014 — Founded

Opened our first 2,000 sqft facility in Pune.

2017 — Expansion

Grew to 5 branches across Maharashtra.

2020 — Digital Launch

Launched online classes during the pandemic, reaching 20k members.

2024 — Nation-wide

Now present in 8 cities with 60,000+ members and counting.
