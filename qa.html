How many protein grams per kg of body weight should I consume to build muscle?

I'm 75 kg and trying to build lean muscle. What's the optimal daily protein intake?

For muscle building, aim for 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of body weight daily. For you at 75 kg, that's 120–165g/day. Distribute this across 4–5 meals. Lean sources like chicken, eggs, paneer, legumes, and whey protein are excellent choices. Consistency over weeks matters more than hitting the exact number on any single day.

👤 Priya K. 4 days ago Weight Loss

Should I do cardio before or after weights for fat loss?

I want to lose fat but preserve muscle. Does the order of cardio and weights matter?

For fat loss with muscle preservation, do weights first, then cardio. Strength training depletes glycogen stores, so your subsequent cardio session burns more fat. If your goal is purely cardiovascular endurance, reverse the order. Keep cardio sessions at moderate intensity (60–70% max heart rate) for 20–30 mins post-lifting for best fat-burning results.

How do I fix muscle imbalances between my left and right side?

My right arm is noticeably bigger than my left. How should I address this?

Switch to unilateral (single-limb) exercises like dumbbell curls, single-arm rows, and single-leg lunges. Always start with your weaker side and match the same reps on your stronger side. Avoid using the stronger side to compensate. Give it 8–12 weeks of consistent training — imbalances typically resolve naturally with unilateral focus. Don't neglect sleep and nutrition either.

👤 Sneha T. 1 week ago Nutrition

What should I eat before a morning workout on an empty stomach?

I train at 6 AM and I'm not sure if I should eat before or work out fasted.

For sessions under 60 minutes, fasted training can work well for fat burning. If you prefer to fuel up, have something light 30–45 minutes before — a banana with peanut butter, a small bowl of oats, or a protein shake. Avoid heavy meals. Post-workout, prioritise protein + carbs within 60 minutes to aid recovery.

👤 Vikram D. 10 days ago Recovery

How do I reduce DOMS (delayed onset muscle soreness) after intense workouts?

My legs are extremely sore 2 days after leg day. Is this normal? How can I speed up recovery?

DOMS is completely normal — it peaks 24–48 hours post-workout. To reduce it: active recovery (light walk, cycling), cold showers or ice baths, foam rolling / massage, adequate sleep (7–9 hrs), and stay hydrated. Eating anti-inflammatory foods (berries, turmeric, omega-3s) also helps. Don't skip the next workout — light movement accelerates recovery.
